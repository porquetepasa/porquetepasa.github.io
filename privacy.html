Privacy Policy — PorQueTePasa Publisher Last updated: 6 November 2026 1. Who uses it. PorQueTePasa Publisher is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @porquetepasa. It has no other users. 2. Data we access. With the owner's authorization, the tool uses the TikTok API to read the basic account information (user.info.basic) and to upload the owner's own videos to their inbox (video.upload). 3. Storage. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. No data from other TikTok users is collected. 4. Deletion. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions) and delete the stored tokens from their computer. 5. Contact. For any question, contact the account owner at the email listed on the TikTok profile @porquetepasa.
